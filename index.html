Phone Connection & Setup Guide

Connect your phone directly to your computer or to another phone using a USB-C cable.

1

Enable Developer Options

On your target phone, open Settings > About phone. Tap Build number 7 times until Developer mode is activated.

2

Turn on USB Debugging

Go to Settings > Developer options and toggle USB debugging ON.

3

Connect via USB Cable

Plug your phone into your computer using a USB cable. Tap Allow on the debugging prompt.

4

Click Connect WebUSB

Click the Connect WebUSB button above, select your phone in the browser dialog, and manage your packages instantly.
